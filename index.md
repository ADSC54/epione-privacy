# Epione Privacy Policy

Epione is a private personal application used solely by its owner to access and analyze personal WHOOP health and fitness data.

## Data accessed

Epione may access WHOOP data including recovery, sleep, cycles, workouts, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, strain, and body measurements, depending on the permissions granted by the user.

## Purpose

The data is used exclusively for personal health, recovery, fitness, and trend analysis.

## Data sharing

Personal WHOOP data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

## Data storage

Some data may be stored locally on the owner's private systems to allow historical and longitudinal analysis.

## Access and revocation

The user may revoke Epione's access to WHOOP at any time through the WHOOP account settings.

## Contact

This application is intended for private personal use only.
